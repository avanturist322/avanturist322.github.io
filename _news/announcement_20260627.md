Our preprint **μVLA: On Recurrent Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation in VLA Models** is now available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12497)! We study how recurrent memory lets Vision-Language-Action models solve partially observable manipulation tasks that require remembering information across long horizons.
