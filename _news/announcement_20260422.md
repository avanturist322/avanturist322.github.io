From April 23 to April 27 I will be at [ICLR 2026](https://iclr.cc/) in Rio de Janeiro, presenting four of our papers. If you are around, come by — happy to chat about memory in RL, VLA, Robotics, and anything in between.

- **Thursday, April 23** — Poster Session 1, Pavilion 3, Poster **P3-#1721**
  *Unraveling the Complexity of Memory in RL Agents: an Approach for Classification and Evaluation* — [arXiv:2412.06531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06531)

- **Friday, April 24** — Poster Session 4, Pavilion 3, Poster **P3-#1904**
  *ELMUR: External Layer Memory with Update/Rewrite for Long-Horizon RL* — [arXiv:2510.07151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07151)

- **Saturday, April 25** — Poster Session 5, Pavilion 4, Poster **P4-#5208**
  *Memory, Benchmark & Robots (MIKASA): A Benchmark for Solving Complex Tasks with Reinforcement Learning* — [arXiv:2502.10550](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10550)

- **Saturday, April 25** — Poster Session 6, Pavilion 3, Poster **P3-#1605**
  *Recurrent Action Transformer with Memory (RATE)* — [arXiv:2306.09459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09459)
